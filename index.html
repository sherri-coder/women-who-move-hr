Our Mission

We Amplify
Her Voice

Women Who Move Hampton Roads exists to shine a spotlight on the extraordinary women building businesses, leading nonprofits, and strengthening communities across our region — because visibility is power.

We believe that when women are seen, celebrated, and connected, entire communities rise. Our platform is where those stories live.

Who We Are

Born in Hampton Roads.
Built for Her.

We are a community platform, a storytelling movement, and a celebration of the women who show up — day in and day out — to make Hampton Roads stronger, smarter, and more equitable.

Whether she's running a corporation, running a nonprofit, or running her block, she belongs here.
